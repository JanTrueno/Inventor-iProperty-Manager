Third-party material used by this add-in
========================================

Autodesk Weave icons
--------------------
The section chevrons (caret-down-xs, caret-right-xs) come from the @weave-design/icons package (version 1.1.0) of
Autodesk's Weave design system, https://github.com/Autodesk/hig (https://www.npmjs.com/package/@weave-design/icons),
licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

The SVG sources were rendered to PNG at 16, 24 and 32 px in a light and a dark colour (src/Assets/Weave). No other
change was made to the artwork.

Inventor interface icons
------------------------
The ribbon button uses the icon of Inventor's own iProperties command, read at run time from the running Inventor.
Nothing from Inventor is copied into or shipped with this add-in.
