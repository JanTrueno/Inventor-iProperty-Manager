Third-party material used by this add-in
========================================

Autodesk Weave icons
--------------------
Some interface icons (edit, caret-down-xs, caret-right-xs, close-xs, close-s) come from
the @weave-design/icons package (version 1.1.0) of Autodesk's Weave design system, https://github.com/Autodesk/hig
(https://www.npmjs.com/package/@weave-design/icons), licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0:
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

The SVG sources were rendered to PNG at 16, 24 and 32 px in a light and a dark colour (src/Assets/Weave). No other
change was made to the artwork.

Inventor interface icons
------------------------
The select arrow in the face fields, and the icons you choose for the spacing toggles and the Edit button, are read at run time from the resource DLLs of the user's own Autodesk Inventor
installation (InvImages_*.dll / InvImagesDark_*.dll). Nothing from those files is copied into or shipped with this add-in.
